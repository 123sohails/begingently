Visual Guides

🙏

Prayer Positions Visual Guide

Interactive visual guide showing each position in prayer with proper posture and focus points.

1
Standing (Qiyam)
Feet shoulder-width, hands folded, eyes looking at prayer place
2
Bowing (Ruku)
Back straight, hands on knees, eyes looking at feet
3
Standing from Ruku
Straighten back completely, arms at sides
4
Prostration (Sujud)
Forehead, nose, palms, knees, toes on ground
5
Sitting (Jalsa)
Sit on left foot, right foot upright, hands on thighs
6
Tashahhud
Point index finger during "la ilaha illallah"

💧

Wudu Steps Visual Guide

Step-by-step visual guide to performing wudu with illustrations and key points to remember.

1
Intention (Niyyah)
Intend to perform wudu for purification
2
Wash Hands
3 times up to wrists, between fingers
3
Rinse Mouth
3 times, swish water thoroughly
4
Clean Nose
3 times, inhale and exhale water
5
Wash Face
3 times from forehead to chin
6
Wash Arms
3 times up to elbows, right then left
7
Wipe Head
Once from front to back
8
Wash Feet
3 times up to ankles, right then left
