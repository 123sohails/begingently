🙏 Salah Guide

Complete prayer guidance based on Quran and authentic Sunnah

Preparation (Wudu)

Quranic Evidence

"O you who have believed, when you rise to [perform] prayer, wash your faces and your forearms to the elbows and wipe over your heads and wash your feet to the ankles."

Quran 5:6
  • 1
    Intention (Niyyah) - "I intend to perform wudu for prayer"
  • 2
    Say "Bismillah" - "In the name of Allah"
  • 3
    Wash hands - 3 times up to wrists, between fingers
  • 4
    Rinse mouth - 3 times, swish thoroughly
  • 5
    Clean nose - 3 times, inhale and exhale water
  • 6
    Wash face - 3 times from forehead to chin
  • 7
    Wash arms - 3 times up to elbows, right then left
  • 8
    Wipe head - Once from front to back
  • 9
    Wash feet - 3 times up to ankles, right then left

Hadith Evidence

"Whoever performs wudu perfectly, his sins will come out from his body, even from under his nails."

Sahih Muslim, Book 2, Hadith 436

Intention (Niyyah)

Hadith Evidence

"Actions are judged by intentions, so each man will have what he intended."

Sahih Bukhari, Book 1, Hadith 1

The intention must be made in the heart before starting prayer. For example:

  • 🕌
    Fajr prayer: "I intend to pray 2 rakat Fajr for Allah"
  • ☀️
    Dhuhr prayer: "I intend to pray 4 rakat Dhuhr for Allah"
  • 🌅
    Asr prayer: "I intend to pray 4 rakat Asr for Allah"
  • 🌇
    Maghrib prayer: "I intend to pray 3 rakat Maghrib for Allah"
  • 🌙
    Isha prayer: "I intend to pray 4 rakat Isha for Allah"

Steps of Prayer

Quranic Evidence

"Indeed, I am Allah. There is no deity except Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance."

Quran 20:14
  • 1
    Standing (Qiyam) - Face Qibla, raise hands, say "Allahu Akbar"
  • 2
    Recitation - Recite Surah Al-Fatiha and another surah
  • 3
    Bowing (Ruku) - Say "Subhana Rabbiyal Adheem" 3 times
  • 4
    Standing from Ruku - Say "Sami Allahu liman hamidah"
  • 5
    Prostration (Sujud) - Say "Subhana Rabbiyal A'ala" 3 times
  • 6
    Sitting (Jalsa) - Brief sit between prostrations
  • 7
    Second Prostration - Repeat "Subhana Rabbiyal A'ala"
  • 8
    Tashahhud - Sit and recite tashahhud in final rakat
  • 9
    Salawat - Send blessings upon Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
  • 10
    Salam - Turn head right and left saying "Assalamu Alaikum"

Common Mistakes

⚠️ Common Errors to Avoid

Rushing through prayer, not focusing, incorrect movements, missing obligatory parts

  • ❌
    Rushing recitation - Take time to recite properly
  • ❌
    Looking around - Keep gaze at place of prostration
  • ❌
    Incorrect posture - Straight back in ruku, proper sujud
  • ❌
    Missing wudu - Ensure proper purification before prayer

Sunnah vs Wajib

Action Wajib (Obligatory) Sunnah (Recommended)
Raising hands At takbir (some scholars) Before/after ruku
Reciting Al-Fatiha In every rakat Additional surah after it
Saying "Ameen" After Al-Fatiha Loudly in congregation
Tasbeeh in ruku/sujud 3 times minimum Odd number (3,5,7)
Sitting for tashahhud In final rakat In first rakat too

🌙 Fasting Guide

Ramadan and Sunnah fasts with complete guidance

Ramadan Fasting

Quranic Evidence

"O you who have believed, decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you that you may become righteous."

Quran 2:183
  • 🌅
    Suhoor (Pre-dawn meal) - Eat before dawn, blessed time
  • 🌇
    Iftar (Breaking fast) - At sunset, preferably with dates
  • 🤲
    Intention (Niyyah) - Each night before fasting
  • 📖
    Increased worship - Quran recitation, prayers, charity

What Breaks Fast

⚠️ Invalidators of Fast

These actions break the fast and require making up the day

  • 🍽️
    Eating and drinking - Intentionally consuming anything
  • 🚬
    Smoking - Any form of smoking or inhalation
  • 💉
    Sexual relations - Intimate activity during fasting hours
  • 🤮
    Vomiting - If done intentionally (not if involuntary)
  • 🩸
    Menstruation - For women, automatically breaks fast

Hadith Evidence

"Whoever eats or drinks forgetfully while fasting, should complete his fast, for it is Allah who fed him and gave him drink."

Sahih Bukhari, Book 30, Hadith 167

What Does NOT Break Fast

  • 🦷
    Brushing teeth - With toothpaste, if taste not swallowed
  • 🌡️
    Injections - Medical injections that don't provide nutrition
  • 🩹
    Blood tests - Small amounts for medical purposes
  • 🚿
    Bathing/Showering - Water doesn't break fast if not swallowed
  • 💋
    Kissing - If doesn't lead to arousal (for married couples)

Sehri & Iftar Sunnah

Hadith Evidence

"Eat suhoor, for in suhoor there is blessing."

Sahih Bukhari, Book 30, Hadith 192
  • 🌅
    Delay suhoor - Eat as close to dawn as possible
  • 🤲
    Make intention - For fasting during suhoor
  • 🗣️
    Make dua - At suhoor and iftar times
  • 🌴
    Break with dates - Odd number of dates (1,3,5)
  • 🍹
    Water - Drink water after dates

Sunnah Fasts (Optional)

  • 📅
    Monday & Thursday - Weekly voluntary fasts
  • ⚪
    White days - 13th, 14th, 15th of lunar month
  • 🌙
    6 days of Shawwal - After Ramadan
  • 🕋
    Day of Arafah - 9th Dhul Hijjah (not for Hajjis)
  • 🌸
    Day of Ashura - 10th Muharram

💚 Good Behaviour (Akhlaq)

Islamic character and conduct in daily life

Honesty in Business

Quranic Evidence

"O you who have believed, do not consume one another's wealth unjustly but only [in] lawful business by mutual consent."

Quran 4:29
  • ⚖️
    Fair measurements - Give full measure, don't cheat
  • 🗣️
    Truthful advertising - Don't misrepresent products
  • 💰
    No interest (Riba) - Avoid usury and interest-based transactions
  • 📝
    Fulfill contracts - Honor business agreements

Hadith Evidence

"The truthful and trustworthy businessman is with the Prophets, the truthful, and the martyrs."

Sunan Ibn Majah, Book 12, Hadith 2139

Justice in Dealings

Quranic Evidence

"Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts to whom they are due and when you judge between people to judge with justice."

Quran 4:58
  • ⚖️
    Fair treatment - Treat all parties equally
  • 👂
    Listen to both sides - Hear all perspectives before judging
  • 🤝
    No favoritism - Don't favor based on wealth or status
  • 📜
    Follow evidence - Base decisions on proof, not assumptions

Kindness to Parents

Quranic Evidence

"And your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment. Whether one or both of them reach old age [while] with you, say not to them [so much as], 'uff,' and do not repel them."

Quran 17:23
  • 🗣️
    Respectful speech - Never speak harshly to parents
  • 🙏
    Make dua for them - Pray for their forgiveness and well-being
  • 💸
    Financial support - Provide for parents if needed
  • ⏰
    Spend time - Visit and care for them, especially in old age

Hadith Evidence

"A man's obedience to his parents is considered one of the best deeds, and his disobedience is one of the major sins."

Sunan Ibn Majah, Book 9, Hadith 2087

Respect Elders

Hadith Evidence

"He is not one of us who does not show mercy to our young and respect our elders."

Sunan Abu Dawood, Book 41, Hadith 4934
  • 🪑
    Give precedence - Let elders go first, offer them best seats
  • 👂
    Listen attentively - Pay attention when elders speak
  • 🤲
    Offer help - Assist elders with physical tasks
  • 🗣️
    Polite address - Use respectful language and titles

Helping Neighbors

Hadith Evidence

"Jibril kept advising me to treat neighbors well until I thought he would make neighbors my heirs."

Sahih Bukhari, Book 73, Hadith 44
  • 🍲
    Share food - Offer meals to neighbors
  • 💊
    Check on them - Visit when sick or in need
  • 🔇
    Consideration - Don't disturb with noise or smells
  • 🎁
    Give gifts - Exchange presents on occasions

⚠️ Avoiding Major Sins

Clear guidance on major sins to avoid for spiritual protection

Lying

⚠️ Serious Consequences

Lying leads to hypocrisy and distance from Allah. Truthfulness leads to righteousness.

Quranic Evidence

"Indeed, Allah does not guide one who is a transgressor and a liar."

Quran 40:28
  • 🗣️
    False testimony - Lying in court or legal matters
  • 📝
    Breaking promises - Not keeping commitments
  • 🎭
    Hypocrisy - Pretending to be what you're not
  • 🤥
    Exaggeration - Making things seem bigger than reality

Hadith Evidence

"Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise. A man keeps telling the truth until he becomes a truthful person."

Sahih Bukhari, Book 73, Hadith 96

Stealing

Quranic Evidence

"And the thief, male and female, cut off their hands as a recompense for what they have earned [as] a deterrent from Allah."

Quran 5:38
  • 💰
    Theft - Taking others' property without permission
  • 🏢
    Embezzlement - Misusing company or public funds
  • 🎯
    Deception - Tricking people out of their money
  • 📦
    Breach of trust - Not returning borrowed items

Adultery and Immorality

⚠️ Grave Sin

Adultery destroys families, spreads disease, and leads to spiritual ruin

Quranic Evidence

"And do not approach unlawful sexual intercourse. Indeed, it is ever an immorality and is evil as a way."

Quran 17:32
  • 👫
    Adultery - Sexual relations outside marriage
  • 👁️
    Lowering gaze - Not looking at non-mahram with desire
  • 👗
    Modest dress - Covering appropriately for both genders
  • 🚪
    Privacy - Not being alone with non-mahram

Hadith Evidence

"When a man commits adultery, faith leaves his heart and covers it like a cloud."

Sunan Ibn Majah, Book 31, Hadith 3933

Intoxicants

Quranic Evidence

"O you who have believed, indeed, intoxicants, gambling, [sacrifices on] stone altars, and divining arrows are but defilement from the work of Satan, so avoid it that you may be successful."

Quran 5:90
  • 🍷
    Alcohol - All forms of alcoholic beverages
  • 🚬
    Drugs - All recreational drugs and intoxicants
  • 🎰
    Gambling - All forms of betting and gambling
  • 💊
    Substance abuse - Misusing prescription drugs

Hadith Evidence

"Every intoxicant is khamr, and every khamr is haram. Whoever drinks khamr in this world and dies without repentance will not drink it in the Hereafter."

Sahih Muslim, Book 23, Hadith 4963

🧼 Cleanliness and Modesty

Physical and spiritual purity with Islamic guidelines

Taharah (Purity)

Quranic Evidence

"Indeed, Allah loves those who are constantly repentant and loves those who purify themselves."

Quran 2:222
  • 💧
    Wudu - Ritual purification before prayer
  • 🚿
    Ghusl - Full body purification after certain events
  • 🧻
    Istinja - Cleaning after using the bathroom
  • 👕
    Clean clothes - Wear clean garments for prayer

Clothing Guidelines

  • 👕
    For men - Cover from navel to knees, loose fitting
  • 👗
    For women - Cover entire body except face and hands
  • 👀
    Non-transparent - Clothes should not be see-through
  • ⚠️
    Not imitating - Avoid opposite gender's clothing

Hadith Evidence

"Whoever wears a garment of pride and ostentation in this world, Allah will clothe him in a garment of humiliation on the Day of Resurrection."

Sunan Abu Dawood, Book 32, Hadith 4049

Hygiene Sunnah

  • 🪥
    Miswak - Using teeth stick before prayer and upon waking
  • ✂️
    Trim nails - Keep nails clean and trimmed
  • 🧔
    Grooming - Remove unwanted hair regularly
  • 👃
    Nose cleanliness - Clean nose during wudu
  • 🦶
    Foot care - Wash feet thoroughly, keep clean

✅ Halal & Haram

Complete guide to permissible and forbidden in Islam with authentic evidence

Food and Drink

Quranic Evidence

"He has only forbidden to you dead animals, blood, the flesh of swine, and that which has been dedicated to other than Allah."

Quran 2:173

🟢 Halal Foods

  • 🐄
    Halal meat - Cow, sheep, goat, chicken, duck (properly slaughtered)
  • 🐟
    Seafood - All fish and sea creatures are halal
  • 🌾
    Grains & vegetables - All plant-based foods are halal
  • 🥛
    Dairy products - Milk, cheese, yogurt (from halal animals)
  • 🍯
    Honey & fruits - All natural foods are halal

🔴 Haram Foods

  • 🐷
    Pork - All pig products including bacon, ham, lard
  • 💀
    Dead animals - Animals that died without proper slaughter
  • 🩸
    Blood - Blood and blood products
  • ⚡
    Improperly slaughtered - Meat not slaughtered in Allah's name
  • 🍷
    Alcohol - All intoxicating beverages

Hadith Evidence

"Every intoxicant is haram, and every intoxicant is forbidden."

Sahih Muslim, Book 23, Hadith 4962

Important Principle

"The halal is clear and the haram is clear, but between them are doubtful matters which many people do not know. Whoever guards against doubtful matters keeps his religion and honor blameless."

Sahih Bukhari, Book 76, Hadith 427

Common Questions

  • ❓
    Can I eat at non-Muslim restaurants?
    Yes, if you can verify halal meat or choose vegetarian/seafood options
  • ❓
    Is gelatin halal?
    Only if from halal sources (fish, beef slaughtered properly). Avoid pork gelatin
  • ❓
    Can Muslims work in banks?
    Not in interest-based roles. Islamic banking departments may be permissible
  • ❓
    Is vanilla extract halal?
    Depends on alcohol content. Most commercial vanilla has minimal alcohol and is considered halal
  • ❓
    Can I listen to nasheeds?
    Yes, if they don't contain instruments and have appropriate content

⚠️ When in Doubt

When uncertain about halal/haram status of something, it's better to avoid it. Consult knowledgeable scholars for specific questions.
