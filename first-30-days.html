🕊 First 30 Days as a Muslim

Your gentle journey to building a sustainable Islamic practice. One week at a time.

Your Journey Progress

Complete checklists to track your progress

1

Learn Shahada Meaning

🎯 This Week's Focus

Understand what you believe and why it matters. The Shahada is the foundation of everything.

📋 Your Checklist

  • Memorize the Shahada in Arabic and English
  • Understand "La ilaha illallah" - No god except Allah
  • Understand "Muhammadur rasulullah" - Muhammad is messenger of Allah
  • Say the Shahada daily with reflection and sincerity
  • Read about Prophet Muhammad's life - Basic introduction
  • Write what the Shahada means to you - Personal reflection
  • Share with someone (optional) - If you feel comfortable

💡 Week 1 Wisdom

"The Shahada isn't just words—it's the key that opens the door to Islam. Understand it, feel it, live it."

2

Learn Wudu + 1 Short Surah

🎯 This Week's Focus

Physical purification and connecting with God's words. Wudu cleanses body and soul.

📋 Your Checklist

  • Learn the 8 steps of wudu - Watch videos, read guides
  • Practice wudu daily - Before prayer or just for purification
  • Memorize Surah Al-Fatiha - The opening chapter (7 verses)
  • Learn Surah Al-Ikhlas - Purity of faith (4 verses)
  • Understand the meaning of both surahs in English
  • Recite both surahs daily - After wudu or before sleep
  • Learn what breaks wudu - Common situations

💡 Week 2 Wisdom

"Wudu isn't just washing—it's preparing your heart to meet God. Each drop cleanses more than skin."

3

Pray 1 Prayer Consistently

🎯 This Week's Focus

Build consistency with one prayer. Quality over quantity. Sincerity over perfection.

📋 Your Checklist

  • Choose ONE prayer time - Maghrib (sunset) is recommended
  • Learn the prayer times - Download a prayer time app
  • Master 2-rakat prayer - Basic movements and words
  • Pray your chosen prayer daily - For 7 consecutive days
  • Set phone reminders - 15 minutes before prayer time
  • Create a prayer space - Clean, quiet area in your home
  • Journal your experience - How did prayer feel?

💡 Week 3 Wisdom

"One prayer done with sincerity is better than five done with resentment. Consistency builds faith."

4

Build Routine

🎯 This Week's Focus

Expand your practice and create sustainable habits. Islam becomes part of your daily life.

📋 Your Checklist

  • Add a second prayer - Choose another prayer time
  • Learn morning adhkar - Simple morning remembrances
  • Learn evening adhkar - Simple evening remembrances
  • Start daily gratitude - Thank God for 3 things before sleep
  • Read 5 minutes daily - Quran or Islamic book
  • Connect with one Muslim - Online or in person
  • Plan your next 30 days - What will you continue/grow?

💡 Week 4 Wisdom

"Routine creates rhythm. Rhythm creates peace. Peace creates connection with God."

🌟 You Did It!

30 days ago, you were just starting. Today, you have a foundation.

Remember: This isn't the end—it's the beginning of a beautiful journey.
